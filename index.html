Ayat & Akal privacy policy

Effective October 30, 2026. Publisher: Eden Bound LLC, Topeka, Kansas, USA. Contact: info@glolaw.org.

Ayat & Akal collects no data.

  • The app has no accounts and no sign-in.
  • It has no ads, analytics, crash reporting, or tracking.
  • It doesn't connect to the internet. All of its content, including the Qur'an text, is inside the app when you install it. The Android version doesn't ask your phone for internet access at all, so it has no way to send anything.
  • What you save and the settings you choose stay on your phone. They're never sent to us or to anyone else. If you delete the app, they're deleted with it.

The app reaches outside itself only in these cases, and only when you tap the button:

  • Donate opens a payment page in your browser. That service's privacy policy applies there.
  • Share opens your phone's share sheet so you can send an entry through an app you choose. That app's privacy policy applies there.
  • Website links on the About screen open in your browser.

Notifications. If you turn on the daily notification, your phone schedules it locally. No server is involved, and we never learn that you turned it on.

Children. The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes. If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted here and inside the app before it takes effect.
